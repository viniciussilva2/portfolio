About

I am a Systems Analyst at the Federation of Industries of the State of Bahia (FIEB), in Salvador, Brazil. I work on the support and evolution of enterprise systems in .NET Framework and .NET Core: I analyse the problem, fix it and deliver the improvement in web applications the business uses every day.

I started in IT in 2020 as an apprentice at Indra, inside the Bahia State Department of Finance, where I later worked with Azure DevOps, build and release pipelines and Sonar. Since then I have supported the Mezzow Connect system, built the API behind the "Decisões" menu of the TCM Portal at the Court of Accounts of the Municipalities of Bahia, and developed SICCO (the Accounts Control System) at Netra Tecnologia.

Beyond the .NET ecosystem, I develop with Python and Django. This site is an example: a Django application in a Docker container, published by a CI/CD pipeline.
